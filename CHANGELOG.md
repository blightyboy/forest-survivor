# Changelog

All notable changes to Forest Survivor are documented here.

## 0.2.5-alpha-build

### Achievements & unlocks
- Achievement credit rewards ×10 (80–700)
- Achievements unlock weapons/cards in the level-up pool (existing saves keep earned unlocks)
- Win goals unlock Greed / Devil's Contract / Angel's Blessing; Fully Armed unlocks Extra Hands
- Glossary shows locked weapons/passives and unlock lines on achievements
- Angel's Blessing: +12% max HP per stack (was +30 flat)

### New achievement weapons (6; each with 6 elemental unique forms)
- **Chain & Hook** — orbiting hooks (First Blood)
- **Snare Trap** — ground snares until stepped on (Trail Scout)
- **Fishing Pole** — reel beasts toward you; more targets per level (Forest Chronicler)
- **Lightning in a Bottle** — chain lightning jumps (Boss Hunter)
- **Flash Bomb** — AoE stun, tiny damage (Night Watch)
- **Poison Bottle** — green ooze pools that DoT beasts (Siege Breaker)

### Glossary & polish
- Glossary icons for variants, elements, boss moves, species (in-game animal art), power-ups, world, achievements
- Assault/raid species pills no longer inherit banner text-shadow (doubled labels)
- Screen shake amplitude halved (0–100% option unchanged)

## 0.2.4-alpha-build

### Landmarks & world
- World POIs: dens, supply wagons, shrines, rich veins (press **E** nearby)
- Landmarks reshuffle each morning with a “forest has shifted” banner
- Rich veins scatter gold chunks (no longer convert nearby rocks to ore)
- Biome patches (clearing / woods / pine) with different wildlife spawn tables
- Run mutators on the main menu (Savage, No Grace, Glass, Siege, Scarcity)

### Win goals & save
- Optional win goals: Endless, Easy (6 nights), Medium (12), Hard (18)
- Final gauntlet night; victory badge + bonus credits
- Mid-run checkpoints (dusk / dawn / pause / periodic); Continue from the menu
- Pause: **Save & Quit** next to Abandon Run

### Combat & telegraphs
- Boss guards spawn with true/mini/day bosses; count and difficulty scale with threat
- Raid / assault banners show species text + color pills
- True-boss moves show as icon chips on spawn banner and boss HUD
- Flame Ring and Caltrop Ring get distinct per-element forms (in-world + card icons)
- Guard dog outdoor form uses infusion skin while hunting

### Codex & achievements
- First kills unlock Glossary species entries (+credits)
- True-boss moves unlock in the codex when faced
- Achievements tab with permanent credit rewards
- Character sheet shows weapons as `(n/5)` with a max-equip note

### Polish
- Camp-scale POI art + proximity **[E]** interact prompt
- Various combat/art fixes from the POI and infusion pass
